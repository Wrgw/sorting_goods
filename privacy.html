Privacy Policy

Goods Puzzle: 3D Sorting Games · Effective date: November 22, 2026

Summary: Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Unity Ads SDKs for advertising, including optional rewarded ads.

Information stored by the game

The game stores gameplay progress, currency, collection progress, and settings in Android app storage using local preferences. This information is used to provide the game and is not uploaded by the gameplay code as a player account. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Advertising services

This build integrates Google Mobile Ads and Unity Ads. These providers may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device information, ad interaction events, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from network information to serve, measure, and prevent fraud in ads. The exact processing depends on provider configuration, consent status, region, and the ad shown. Optional rewarded video is initiated by the player.

See Google Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy for provider practices and available controls.

Permissions and network

The Android application has internet access for advertising and service requests. The game does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise device location in its app manifest.

Children

The game is a general audience puzzle game. Advertising availability and treatment may vary by age, region, and the consent state provided to the advertising SDKs. A parent or guardian with a privacy question can contact the developer through the app store listing.

Retention and choices

Local game data remains on the device until app data is cleared or the app is removed. Advertising providers control their own retention and user choice mechanisms as described in their policies and in platform settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be revised when the app or its SDK configuration changes. For privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on the official app store page.
